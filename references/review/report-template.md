## Applicable criteria (optional)

|---|---:|---|
| [Applicable criterion] | | |

Only include criteria actually assessed. `M = 4 × applicable assessed criteria`; n/a and not assessed are excluded. Scores do not override any critical finding or prove approval.

## Findings (ordered by user impact)

### [P0–P3] Short, specific finding title
- **Location:** exact frame/component/layer, route/state, or source location.
- **User impact:** affected user/task and concrete consequence.
- **Evidence:** observed/inferred/unknown; artifact kind and revision/build; device/platform, viewport, mode and state; capture/record pointer.
- **Recommendation:** actionable proposed correction; no edits performed by this review.
- **Verification:** repeatable evidence/check and expected result; name platform and states to exercise.
- **Verification status:** evidence-only / not-verified / blocked / verified for the named criterion; no broader pass implied.

- No runtime/native/performance/approval pass implied beyond explicitly exercised evidence.
